# Disclaimer

This repository is intended for educational, defensive and authorized lab use only.

The material published here may include payload analysis, decoding notes, detection logic, sanitized samples, scripts, reports, YARA rules, Sigma rules, Suricata rules and technical documentation.

## Allowed use

This material may be used for:

- Authorized labs
- CTF environments
- Defensive research
- Security training
- Controlled testing environments
- Detection engineering
- Payload decoding
- IOC extraction
- Defensive reporting

## Not allowed

This material must not be used for:

- Unauthorized access
- Attacks against third-party systems
- Credential theft
- Persistence
- Evasion
- Malware deployment
- Data theft
- Service disruption
- Targeting systems without explicit permission
- Publishing or handling live malware unsafely
- Sharing stolen data, dumps, credentials or tokens

## Payload handling rules

Do not publish:

- Live malware binaries
- Weaponized payloads
- Real credentials
- Tokens
- Session cookies
- Private keys
- Dumps
- Personal data
- Private infrastructure details
- Sensitive screenshots
- Internal IP mappings unless intentionally documented as lab-only

Use safe alternatives:

- Hashes
- Redacted logs
- Sanitized payloads
- Neutralized samples
- Detection rules
- Behavioral descriptions
- Mitigation guidance

## Responsibility

Users are responsible for how they use this material.

Do not run payloads, scripts, rules or commands against systems you do not own or do not have explicit permission to test.
